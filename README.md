# Mega Meow-Mart

**SCALE SCALE SCALE!**

**Mega Meow-Mart** is a cat mangement-simulation game where you try to ecscape your ever-growing debt by building and expanding your supermarket empire. As the manager of Mega Meow-Mart you'll hire cat stockers & cashiers, build shelves & counters, and order product to sell as you plunge deeper and deeper into debt.

Will you build the purrrrfect Mega Meow-Mart? Or will you have to lose your store to forclosure? Time is money in the feline world, and you'll have to scale quickly to make your supermarket dream pawsible!

## Controls

Movement: WASD/ArrowKeys

Drop box/fill shelf: Space/click

Zoom in/out: Scroll wheel

Build Mode: B

Hiring Mode: H

Buying Menu: R

Pause: esc

## Notes:

- If the font looks weird, change the page zoom (80% on 1440p looks good, press ctrl +/- to change page zoom)
- To expand shop, enter build mode (B) and click the white arrow in the direction you wish to expand
- You can hold shift in the buy menu to buy multiple of an item
- Keep all items stocked at all times if possible
- Try not to place items on cats
- You can't fire cats (they have rights, you know)
- You can't delete items/boxes (they have rights, you know)
